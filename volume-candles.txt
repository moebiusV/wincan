Volume candles: the width rule measured from KuCoin's chart
==========================================================

Reference: a screenshot of KuCoin's ZECUSDT perpetual chart, 1-minute candles, volume candles on (the chart is TradingView's), at 2026-11-20 09:23 UTC.
Method: every candle's high and low were taken from KuCoin's own klines for those minutes (kline/query, granularity 1), turned into pixel rows with the price
axis of the screenshot (32 pixels to the dollar), the candle found in the picture by its wick, and its body width measured on the rows of its body.  Volume is
the kline's volume in lots.

Width of the body, in pixels, against the volume in lots (51 candles located):
   width  7 : volumes 799 .. 2948, median 2129      (typical candles; never thinner)
   width  9 : volumes 2886 .. 3349
   width 11 : volumes 3807 .. 3971
   width 15 : volume 5436
   width 19 : volumes 8004 and 8228
   widest runs in the picture: 24 pixels (the largest volume in view: 11991 lots)
Volumes on show: median 2234, mean 2775, minimum 545, maximum 11991.

A first fit, on this screenshot alone, was a square-root law against the median volume.

A second screenshot (ZECUSDT perpetual, 1 minute, volume candles, 2026-11-20 10:17 UTC; the candles located by their wicks against KuCoin's klines, the price
axis calibrated by search to 21.25 pixels to the dollar) gave 37 more candles:
   width  7 : 29 candles, volumes 918 .. 2855
   width  9 : 3094       width 11 : 3635       width 15 : 5017, 5084, 5563       width 19 : 7976       width 23 : 11455 (the largest in view)
The two agree with each other, and the step from 7 to 9 comes at 2870 lots, between 2855 (width 7) and 2886 (width 9).  The mean volume on show was 2690 .. 2830 in
the two, so the width starts to grow at about the mean volume; and it grows with the logarithm of the volume, not with a power: from 3635 to 5084 to 7976 to 11455 lots
(ratios of 1.4, 1.6, 1.4) the width goes up by 4 pixels each time.

The rule now used (88 candles located in all, widths within the odd-number quantisation of KuCoin's):
      width / standard = max(1, 1.10 + 1.57 * ln(volume / mean volume on show))      as an odd number of pixels
i.e. about 7.7 + 11 ln(volume / mean) pixels where the standard is 7 pixels; it reaches 3.4 times the standard width at 4.3 times the mean volume.  Correction (2026-11-24, the author): the candles do not overlap in KuCoin's chart.  Each keeps its own width, side by side with the standard gap, so the wide ones take room and fewer candles are on show; the spacing of the
candles is therefore not even.  More reference screenshots (other pairs, zoom levels, a quiet stretch) would make it closer still.
